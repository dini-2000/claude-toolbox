## 01
# What we set out to do

<!-- notes: Three parts tonight: how the scheme worked, what the panels made, and the plan for a second round. -->

---

###### The plan
## One survey, one installer, one price for every roof

- We asked three installers to quote for the whole street at once
- Each home chose its own panel count from the same price list
- The co-op paid nothing: every household bought its own system
- Installs ran over six weeks, two homes a week

<!-- notes: The idea came from number 14, who had a quote of 1.4 million rupees for a single roof. Buying as a group got the per-panel price down by about a fifth. -->

## 02
# What the panels made

<!-- notes: These are whole-year figures, so they include the wet months of May and June, when output dropped by about a third. -->

---

###### The year in figures
## Eighteen roofs, one good year

- **18** homes with panels
- **61 kW** of panels in all
- **74 MWh** made in twelve months
- **-38%** on the average bill

<!-- notes: The 74 megawatt hours is the total from the inverter apps. The bill figure is the average across the 15 homes that shared both years' bills. Three homes didn't, so they're left out of it. -->

---

The cheapest panel on the street is the one we bought together.

<!-- notes: The group price came to about 52,000 rupees a panel, fitted. The best single-home quote anyone on the street got was 64,000. -->

## Bigger roofs paid back faster

| Home size | Homes | Panels each | Payback |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| Small | 6 | 6 | 5.8 years |
| Medium | 8 | 10 | 5.1 years |

<!-- notes: Payback here is the system price divided by the year's saving on the bill. It assumes tariffs stay where they are, which they probably won't. -->

---

###### What worked
## Doing it as a street made it easy

### One contract
Every home signed the same terms, so nobody had to read three quotes alone.

### One installer
The crew learned the street's roofs and finished the last homes in half the time.

### One chat group
Problems went to the group first, and most were answered by a neighbour.

<!-- notes: The contract was the installer's standard one, with two changes our lawyer neighbour at number 3 asked for: a ten-year roof-leak warranty and a fixed install date per home. -->

---
