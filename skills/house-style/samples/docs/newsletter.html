Annual meeting

Members back the new plot fee and the polytunnel

Sixty-two members came to the annual meeting on 14 November, the most since the garden opened. They voted 51 to 9 for a plot fee of $55 in 2027, with two abstentions.

The extra $5 a plot pays for the composting toilet by the east gate, due in April, and helps towards a polytunnel for seedlings in the summer. Raised beds stay at $30, and members who find the fee hard can still ask the treasurer for a reduced rate in confidence.

The meeting also elected two new committee members, Tom Price and Leila Haddad, and thanked Margaret Osei, who steps down after nine years as treasurer. Margaret will keep running the plant sale.

Minutes of the meeting are on the shed noticeboard and on the website. The full season report is there too, with the accounts.

In the garden this winter

December. Cover bare beds with cardboard or leaves to keep the soil from washing away. Check stored potatoes and onions each week.

January. Order seed potatoes and onion sets. Clean pots and trays for spring. Prune apple and pear trees on dry days.

February. Chit early potatoes on a windowsill. Sow broad beans under cover, and spread compost on the beds you will use first.
