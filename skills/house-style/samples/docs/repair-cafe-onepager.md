# Easton Repair Café {-}

Bring a broken toaster, lamp, jacket or bike. Volunteers fix it with you, for free, on the first Saturday of every month at the Old Library, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. {.lead}

::: keyfigs
- **1,140** things mended since 2023
- **68%** of items fixed on the day
- **32** volunteer fixers
:::

::: cols-2
## What we fix

Small electricals, lamps, clothes and bags, bikes, toys, furniture and jewellery. We can't take gas appliances, microwaves or anything that needs more than one person to lift.

## What it costs

Nothing. A donation jar helps pay for spare parts and the room, and tea and cake are $1. Parts we have to buy are charged at cost, and we always ask first.
:::

## How a visit works

::: steps
1. **Book in.** Give your name and the item at the front desk, and take a number.
2. **Sit with a fixer.** They show you how they find the fault, and you help with the repair.
3. **Take it home.** If it can't be fixed on the day, the fixer tells you why and where to go next.
:::

> [!TIP] Volunteer with us
> We need fixers for sewing, small electricals and bikes, and people to run the front desk. Come to any session and ask for Rosa, or email fix@eastonrepair.example.
