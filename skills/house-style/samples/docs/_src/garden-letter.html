Water storage grant 2025/114, end-of-project report

Dear Ms Shah,

Thank you for the city's grant of $4,000 towards water storage at the garden. The work is finished, and this letter is our end-of-project report, as the grant terms ask.

We fitted four rain tanks in February, holding 20,000 litres in all. Between June and September the garden used about a third less mains water than in the same months of 2025, and our water bill came in $300 under budget. The tanks were full again by the end of October.

The tanks and fitting cost $3,900. We would like to keep the remaining $100 for two new tap fittings by the raised beds, if the council agrees. If not, we will return it with the receipts.

I have enclosed the receipts, the meter readings for 2025 and 2026, and four photographs of the finished work. Members are welcome to show you the tanks on any Saturday morning, or on a weekday if that suits you better.

Thank you again for your help with this project.

Yours sincerely,

Helen BarkerSecretary, Northside Community Garden
