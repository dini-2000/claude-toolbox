Good to know

Entry
Free. Games cost Rs 50 a turn, for new park benches.
Food
Tokens from the desk by the main gate; the tent takes no cash.
First aid
The tent beside the desk, with two nurses on duty all day.
Lost children
Bring them to the desk, and we call their family on the speakers.
Parking
The school grounds on Lake Road, from 8 a.m. The park has no parking of its own.
If it rains
Games move to the school hall. We post any change in the association's WhatsApp group by 7 a.m.
Prizes
The winning tug of war team takes home a cake from Silva's Bakery, and every child who runs a race gets a medal.
