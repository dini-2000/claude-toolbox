Tide Studio Prepared for Ana Rossi, owner, Harbour Bakery

Summary

Harbour Bakery takes about 140 phone orders a week, mostly for cakes and bread on Saturday mornings. Each call takes four minutes on average, at the busiest hour of the day.

We propose a new website with online ordering and collection slots, built in twelve weeks for $18,400. Customers choose a cake, a size and a collection time, and pay when they order. The bakery sees each day's orders on one screen and prints a bake list at 5 a.m.

What you asked for

Ana asked for three things when we met on 21 October.

  1. Fewer calls on Saturdays. Orders by phone tie up one person from 7 to 11 a.m.
  2. Fewer cakes left on the shelf. About one ordered cake in twenty is never collected, and most of those were never paid for.
  3. A site that looks like the shop. The current site was built in 2017 and shows last year's prices.

What we found

We read the order book for September and October and spent a Saturday morning in the shop.

Most calls ask the same few questions: is a cake available, what sizes it comes in, and when it can be collected. These answers can all sit on the website. Calls about wedding and birthday cakes take longerThe bakery made 31 custom cakes in September and October. Each needed at least two calls and a visit., and we would keep those as calls, with a short form to book one.

Uncollected cakes cost the bakery about $2,600 a year at full price. Paying at the time of ordering should end most of that.

How we would work

Four stages

  1. Discover. Two workshops with the shop team, a review of the order book, and a site map for you to sign off.
  2. Design. Page designs for phone and desktop, shown to six regular customers before we build anything.
  3. Build. The site, the order screen and the payments, tested by the team on a quiet weekday.
  4. Launch and support. A soft launch to the mailing list, then the full launch, with eight weeks of support.

What we need from you

One hour a week from Ana or the shop manager, photos of the cakes (we can arrange a photographer for $650), and access to the bakery's payment account.

What you get

  • A website you can edit yourselves: prices, opening hours, the cake list and the photos.
  • Online ordering with collection slots in fifteen-minute steps, and a daily limit per cake so the bakery never takes more orders than it can bake.
  • A one-screen view of the day's orders, and a bake list to print each morning.
  • Payment by card, Apple Pay and Google PayCard payments cost about 1.5% plus 20 cents per order with the payment provider we suggest. The bakery pays this directly..
  • Training for three members of staff, and a short written guide.
