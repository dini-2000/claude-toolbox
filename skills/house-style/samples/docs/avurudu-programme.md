# Programme {-}

::: agenda
- **Morning**
- **8.30** Gates open | Kiribath, kavum and tea at the food tent
- **9.15** Lighting the hearth | Main stage. Our oldest member lights the year's first fire
- **9.45** Raban drumming | Main stage. Six teams, judged on rhythm and timing
- **10.30** Children's races | Lawn A. Lime and spoon and sack races, ages 5 to 12
- **11.30** Pot breaking (kana mutti) | Lawn B. Blindfolded, three swings. Ages 13 and over
- **12.30** Lunch | Food tent. Menu and prices on the back page
- **Afternoon**
- **1.30** Tug of war | Lawn A. Teams of eight; sign up at the desk by 1 p.m.
- **2.30** Pillow fight on the pole (kotta pora) | Lawn B. Two players on a log, one hand behind the back
- **3.15** The elephant's eye | Lawn A. Blindfolded, mark the eye on the drawing
- **4.00** Avurudu Kumari and Kumaraya | Main stage. Traditional dress and a two-minute talk
- **4.45** Prizes and close | Main stage
:::

# Good to know {-}

::: facts
- **Entry**: Free. Games cost Rs 50 a turn, for new park benches.
- **Food**: Tokens from the desk by the main gate; the tent takes no cash.
- **First aid**: The tent beside the desk, with two nurses on duty all day.
- **Lost children**: Bring them to the desk, and we call their family on the speakers.
- **Parking**: The school grounds on Lake Road, from 8 a.m. The park has no parking of its own.
- **If it rains**: Games move to the school hall. We post any change in the association's WhatsApp group by 7 a.m.
- **Prizes**: The winning tug of war team takes home a cake from Silva's Bakery, and every child who runs a race gets a medal.
:::
